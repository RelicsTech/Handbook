Section I: Responding To Tickets

Ticket Claiming System

Important Notice:

Our ticket system requires you to actively claim tickets before responding. This ensures clear responsibility and prevents duplicate responses.

Claiming Tickets:

  • When a ticket is opened, you must click the "Claim" button in the Tickety embed to take responsibility for it
  • Claiming a ticket makes it so ONLY YOU (besides the client) can type in that ticket channel
  • CRUCIAL: If you need to leave a ticket, you MUST:
    1. Go to the pinned embed and click "Unclaim"
    2. Notify in #specialist-chat that the ticket needs reassignment
    3. Provide context to the next specialist taking over

Supervisor Requirement:

  • All Junior Staff must run /supervisor at the start of every ticket
  • This adds a Senior Specialist to oversee your work and provide guidance
  • Even after promotion, you may still request supervisors for complex cases

General Guidelines

When responding to a ticket, keep all communication strictly inside of the ticket. Never ask for any personal information from the user.

Important Notice:

If the user's problem involves their physical computer/laptop and you need to guide them to open it and fix the issue, you must copy and paste the following message to them. They must agree before you continue:

"By opening one of these tickets you agree that although we will never give you false information, we do not take any responsibility if your computer breaks or is damaged in the process of seeking support. Say I Agree/Disagree To Continue"

If they agree, you may proceed with the support. If they disagree, you cannot continue with physical troubleshooting.

Section II: Tech Support/Troubleshooting Tickets

Your goal is to try your best to help them resolve their issue using your technical knowledge and the resources available to you.

Section V: Ticket Commands

Available Ticket Commands

These commands can be used in any ticket channel to manage and interact with the ticket system.

/ticket add

Adds a new person to the ticket. Useful when you need assistance from another specialist or want to include a manager.

/ticket add @username

/ticket remove

Removes someone from the ticket. Use this when a specialist is no longer needed in the conversation.

/ticket remove @username

/ticket claim

Claims the ticket for yourself. This marks you as the responsible specialist handling this ticket.

/ticket claim

/ticket priority

Changes the priority of the ticket. Priorities help organize workload and identify urgent cases.

/ticket priority [low/medium/high]

Priority Indicators:
Low Priority - Standard issue
Medium Priority - Needs attention soon
High Priority - Urgent issue

/ticket rename

Renames the ticket to something more descriptive. Helps with organization and quick identification.

/ticket rename "New Ticket Name"

/ticket requestclose

Requests the ticket opener if they want to close the ticket. Use when you believe the issue is resolved.

/ticket requestclose

/ticket close

Closes the ticket immediately. Only use when absolutely certain the issue is resolved.

/ticket close

/ticket info

Displays information about the ticket including creation date, participants, and current status.

/ticket info

/ticket notes

Adds private notes to the ticket that only staff can see. Useful for internal documentation.

/ticket notes "Your private note here"

/supervisor

Adds a Senior Specialist or Head Specialist to the ticket for additional support or oversight.

/supervisor

Section VI: Specialist Ranks

Rank Progression System

Our specialist team operates on a tiered ranking system that recognizes experience, dedication, and performance. Each rank comes with additional responsibilities and permissions.

Junior Specialist

Requirements:

Entry-level position obtained after successfully applying and being accepted into our staff team.

Purpose:

This probationary period allows you to demonstrate your technical knowledge, communication skills, and commitment to helping our community.

Permissions:
  • Basic ticket management
  • Access to support resources
  • Standard response capabilities

Specialist

Promotion Criteria:

Awarded to Junior Specialists who have consistently provided high-quality support, maintained excellent response times, and received positive feedback from users.

Responsibilities:

Full ticket handling capabilities, expected to handle more complex issues, and serve as a role model for Junior Specialists.

Additional Permissions:
  • Priority ticket assignment
  • Extended ticket management commands
  • Ability to mentor Junior Specialists

Senior Specialist

Achievement:

Earned by Specialists who have shown exceptional dedication, often after several months of outstanding service and leadership within the team.

Leadership Role:

Senior Specialists help guide the support team, assist with training, and handle the most complex technical issues.

Enhanced Permissions:
  • Priority override capabilities
  • Advanced moderation tools
  • Ability to assist with staff training
  • Can fill out Junior Staff Observation Forms to evaluate readiness for promotion

Head Specialist

Exclusive Position:

Only one Head Specialist serves at any time, selected from our most experienced and trusted Senior Specialists.

Primary Duties:
  • Oversee all staff promotions and demotions
  • Review and process staff applications
  • Develop and conduct training programs
  • Ensure quality control across all support interactions
Administrative Privileges:
  • Full ticket system control
  • Staff management capabilities
  • Policy implementation authority
  • Final approval on all Junior Staff Observation Forms
  • Direct reporting to server administration

Ticket Management Permissions

Senior Specialists Can:
  • Reassign tickets between specialists
  • Provide guidance on complex technical issues
  • Assist with training new staff members
Head Specialist Can:
  • Perform all Senior Specialist ticket functions
  • Audit ticket history and specialist performance
  • Implement system-wide ticket policy changes

Section VII: Policies

Use of AI

While AI tools can be valuable resources for assisting with technical support, they should be used responsibly and in moderation.

Guidelines:

  • AI may be used as a supplementary tool for research and troubleshooting
  • All responses should be reviewed and personalized before being shared with users
  • Direct copying of AI-generated responses is prohibited
  • Your own technical knowledge should always be the primary resource

Remember that while AI can provide helpful suggestions, our goal is to offer human-to-human support with personal attention to each user's unique situation.

Personal Information Policy

Specialists must refrain from asking users for personal information including but not limited to:

Important Notice:

In order to preserve the user's privacy and uphold strict ethical standards, specialists must avoid collecting any personally identifiable information unless absolutely necessary and explicitly consented to.

Prohibited Information Requests:

  • Full name or any identifying aliases
  • Physical address or location details
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Identification numbers (SSN, driver's license, etc.)
  • Financial information
  • Any other information that could be used to identify or contact the user

Specialists must prioritize user privacy and only request information that is strictly necessary for the technical support task at hand. Any requests for personal information must be justified by absolute necessity and require explicit user consent.

Promotions

Our promotion system is designed to recognize and reward staff members who demonstrate consistent excellence in their roles.

Junior Specialist → Specialist

  • After taking several tickets and remaining active, Senior Staff+ will schedule an "Observation"
  • Senior Staff will complete an observation form evaluating your performance
  • Promotion decisions are based on this formal evaluation
  • Demonstrate consistent activity and quality responses
  • Complete an introduction in #specialist-intros including:
    • Your name/nickname
    • Where you're from
    • Your areas of technical expertise
    • Why you joined the support team

Specialist → Senior Specialist

  • Consistently high user satisfaction ratings
  • Demonstrated leadership within the team
  • Active participation in community discussions
  • Willingness to mentor junior staff members
  • Consistent activity over an extended period

Note:

Promotions beyond Specialist are evaluated based on overall contribution to the community, not just quantitative metrics. Leadership qualities, technical expertise, and dedication to helping others are all considered.

Inactivity Policy

To maintain an active and responsive support team, we require all staff members to remain engaged with our community.

Activity Requirements:

  • If you do not participate in the server in a 14 day period without warning, you can be striked, demoted, or fired
  • Participation in community discussions is encouraged
  • Extended absences should be communicated to leadership

Example Scenario:

A staff member hasn't responded to any tickets or participated in server discussions for 3 weeks without notice. After attempts to contact them go unanswered, they would be subject to removal from the staff team.

We understand that real life takes priority, so communication about availability is always appreciated and will be taken into consideration.

Three Strike System

To maintain high standards of professionalism and service quality, we employ a three-strike system for policy violations.

First Strike

  • Issued for first-time violations including:
    • Breaking server rules as staff
    • Unprofessional behavior
    • Clear misuse of AI (direct copying)
  • Results in a formal warning
  • Lasts for 14 days

Second Strike

  • Issued for repeating the same violations that earned the first strike
  • May result in temporary restrictions
  • Lasts for 14 days
  • Requires review with leadership

Third Strike

  • Issued for continued violations after previous strikes
  • Results in removal from the staff team
  • May be appealed after 30 days

Important:

Severe violations may skip the strike system entirely and result in immediate removal at the discretion of leadership. All strikes expire after 14 days if no further violations occur.

Section VIII: Training Guide

Welcome!

This guide is designed to train junior staff members in reviewing applications, handling tickets, and interacting with the team. Follow each step carefully to ensure consistency and quality.

Step 1: Initial Contact

Paste the following message to the new staff member:

"Hello! Congrats on passing this application. I will give you the handbook to look over. If you have any questions please ask me. Tell me once you've looked over the handbook, and I will continue to the next section of this briefing. https://relicstech.github.io/Handbook/"

Wait for them to confirm they have read the handbook.

Step 2: Initial Questions

Once the new staff member has read the handbook, send the following message:

"If you have any questions please ask our Founder or Co-Founder, Benoopi or Gliive. Or raive_03 (Fat Hampster), a senior specialist. Please provide me with a JPEG or PNG of your Discord profile image so we can include you on the website in https://relicstech.uk/

The amount of tickets that are opened each day depends. Sometimes there are 8 and some days there are none for a few days.

An interview with you will not be needed, but I just want to ask you a few questions before you begin.

I'd like you to list me 5 things you've learned from the handbook. And tell me what you ask people to complete if a PC building form is opened, and where you can view the results. Finally, tell me if you are best at PC building, or tech support or both."

After they answer, forward their responses to @benoopi or @gliive for review.

Step 3: Application Review

Review the new applicant's responses question by question. Ensure that:

  • Responses are correct
  • Responses are of high quality
  • Grammar is correct

There are only two questions. Take both answers and run them through an AI detector (preferably ZeroGPT: https://www.zerogpt.com/). Ensure the responses are accurate and of good quality.

If the applicant passes:
