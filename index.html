Ticket Protocols

The Claiming System

Active Claiming Required

You must click "Claim" on a ticket to take responsibility. This locks the chat so only YOU and the client can type.

How to Claim

T
Tickety BOT Today at 3:42 PM

Welcome to Relic's Tech Support! A specialist will be with you shortly.

Leaving a Ticket

  1. Go to the pinned embed & click "Unclaim".
  2. Notify in #specialist-chat.
  3. Provide context for the next specialist.

Physical Liability Disclaimer

If guiding a user to open their PC or perform physical fixes, you MUST obtain consent. Use the following command to display the disclaimer:

/disclaimer

Tech Support & Troubleshooting

The Workflow

Users fill out a form before the ticket opens. Check the top of the chat for their answers (OS, Issue Type, Description). Use this to jumpstart your diagnosis.

Example Ticket Flow
U
WeeeWooo 4:15 PM
OS: Win 11
Issue: Freezing
My computer keeps freezing randomly after the last update.
S
4:20 PM An19
Hello! I can help with that. Did you check Task Manager to see if any specific program is spiking CPU usage?

Quick Tips

  • 1. Check #Specialist-Resources first.
  • 2. If Needed, Use AI for brainstorming, but verify the info. Since AI Can Be Inaccurate
  • 3. Keep it simple. Start with basics (Restart, Drivers).

Command Reference

Basic Ticket Commands

/ticket claim Essential

Assigns the ticket to you. Required before helping.

/supervisor Required

Pings Senior/Head staff to oversee your ticket. Mandatory for Junior Staff.

/ticket priority [level] Utility

Set urgency: low, medium, high.

/ticket requestclose [reason] [3d] Action

Asks user if they want to close ticket. Must specific "3d" (3 days).

/ticket add @user Management

Adds a new person (staff or user) to the current ticket channel.

/ticket remove @user Management

Removes a user from the ticket.

/ticket rename "Name" Utility

Changes channel name to be more descriptive.

/ticket notes "Text" Internal

Adds a private note visible only to staff.

/ticket close Action

Closes the ticket immediately.

Staff Hierarchy

Junior Specialist

Entry Level

Probationary period. Focus on learning protocols and demonstrating reliability.

Duties: Basic tickets, Standard responses.
Must use /supervisor command.

Specialist

Standard Staff

Earned through quality support and consistency. Handles complex tickets independently.

  • Priority ticket assignment
  • Mentors Junior staff

Senior Specialist

Leadership

Outstanding dedication. Guides the team and handles escalated issues.

Power: Reassign tickets, Moderate chat, Fill observation forms.

Head Specialist

Administration

One position only. Oversees all staff, training, and policy.

Staff Policies

Use of AI

While AI tools can be valuable resources for assisting with technical support, they should be used responsibly and in moderation.

Guidelines

  • AI may be used as a supplementary tool for research and troubleshooting.
  • All responses should be reviewed and personalized before being shared with users.
  • Direct copying of AI-generated responses is prohibited.
  • Your own technical knowledge should always be the primary resource.

Remember that while AI can provide helpful suggestions, our goal is to offer human-to-human support with personal attention to each user's unique situation.

Tipping Policy

We appreciate that users may wish to reward exceptional service. However, to maintain professionalism and fairness, strict guidelines apply to accepting gratuities.

Permitted

  • Accepting a tip only if explicitly offered by the user first.
  • Communication via the ticket channel.
  • Communication via DMs only with prior permission from Senior Staff+ (SR+).

Prohibited

  • Soliciting or asking for a tip.
  • Setting a price for support services.
  • Requesting a specific amount.

Strict Enforcement

Any specialist found requesting tips, setting prices, or negotiating amounts will be issued TWO Strikes on a first offense, and will be subject to IMMIDIATE REMOVAL From the team after the first offense. Our support services are free; tipping is entirely optional and user-initiated.

Personal Information Policy

Specialists must refrain from asking users for personal information including but not limited to:

Important Notice

In order to preserve the user's privacy and uphold strict ethical standards, specialists must avoid collecting any personally identifiable information unless absolutely necessary and explicitly consented to.

Prohibited Information Requests

  • Full name or any identifying aliases
  • Physical address or location details
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Identification numbers (SSN, etc.)
  • Financial information

Specialists must prioritize user privacy and only request information that is strictly necessary for the technical support task at hand. Any requests for personal information must be justified by absolute necessity and require explicit user consent.

Promotions

Our promotion system is designed to recognize and reward staff members who demonstrate consistent excellence in their roles.

Junior Specialist → Specialist

  • After taking several tickets and remaining active, Senior Staff+ will schedule an "Observation".
  • Senior Staff will complete an observation form evaluating your performance.
  • Promotion decisions are based on this formal evaluation.
  • Demonstrate consistent activity and quality responses.
  • Complete an introduction in #specialist-intros including:
    • Your name/nickname
    • Where you're from
    • Your areas of technical expertise
    • Why you joined the support team

Specialist → Senior Specialist

  • Consistently high user satisfaction ratings.
  • Demonstrated leadership within the team.
  • Active participation in community discussions.
  • Willingness to mentor junior staff members.
  • Consistent activity over an extended period.

Note

Promotions beyond Specialist are evaluated based on overall contribution to the community, not just quantitative metrics. Leadership qualities, technical expertise, and dedication to helping others are all considered.

Inactivity Policy

To maintain an active and responsive support team, we require all staff members to remain engaged with our community.

Activity Requirements:

  • If you do not participate in the server in a 14 day period without warning, you can be striked, demoted, or fired.
  • Participation in community discussions is encouraged.
  • Extended absences should be communicated to leadership.

Example Scenario

A staff member hasn't responded to any tickets or participated in server discussions for 3 weeks without notice. After attempts to contact them go unanswered, they would be subject to removal from the staff team.

We understand that real life takes priority, so communication about availability is always appreciated and will be taken into consideration.

Three Strike System

To maintain high standards of professionalism and service quality, we employ a three-strike system for policy violations.

First Strike

Cause: First-time violations (e.g., Breaking rules, Unprofessional behavior, Misuse of AI, Soliciting or Requesting a tip)

Result: Formal warning.

Duration: 14 days.

Second Strike

Cause: Repeating violations, Soliciting or Requesting a tip

Result: Temporary restrictions & Leadership review.

Duration: 14 days.

Third Strike

Cause: Continued violations.

Result: Removal from staff team.

Appeal: After 30 days.

Important

Severe violations may skip the strike system entirely and result in immediate removal at the discretion of leadership. All strikes expire after 14 days if no further violations occur.
